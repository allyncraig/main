# Aramaic Primacy Notes

[Projects Page](index.md)

Aramaic Primacy is a false theory based on the false idea that Aramaic was the only language commonly spoken by the Jews in Judea and Galilee during the time of Jesus and the early apostles, which leads to the equally false conclusion that if Jesus and the disciples only spoke Aramaic, then the original text of the New Testament had to have been written in Aramaic instead of Greek. This false theory also states that the *Peshitta*, an early translation of the New Testament into Aramaic was actually the original New Testament text and that as a consequence, Bible translations should be based on *Peshitta* instead of on Greek texts.

I have studied this theory extensively and have compiled some of my notes into the documents linked below.

**Abbreviations**: In these documents, I commonly use the following abbreviations:

- **AP** : Aramaic Primacy
- **1CP** : First Century Palestine
- **1CPA** : First Century Palestinian Aramaic
- **1CGA** : First Century Galilean Aramaic (Same thing as 1CPA, they are used interchangeably)
- **5CES** : Fifth Century Edessan Syriac
